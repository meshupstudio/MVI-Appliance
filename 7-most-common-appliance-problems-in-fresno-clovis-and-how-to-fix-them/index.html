7 Most Common Appliance Problems in Fresno & Clovis (And How to Fix Them)

Most service calls we get in Fresno and Clovis fall into a short list of recurring problems. Here's what to check before you pick up the phone — and when it's time to call a technician instead.

Quick Answer

Refrigerator cooling issues, washer drainage problems, and clogged dryer vents make up the majority of service calls in Fresno and Clovis. Most start with a simple check — coils, hoses, or filters — before ever needing a technician.

1. Refrigerator Not Cooling

Start with the coils and door seals; if both look fine, the compressor or thermostat may need a professional diagnosis. See our refrigerator maintenance guide for the full routine, or our guide on fridge warm, freezer cold issues if the freezer's fine but the fridge isn't.

2. Washer Won't Drain

Often a clogged drain hose or filter — usually a quick fix once identified. Check that the hose isn't kinked and the filter (usually near the bottom front panel) is clear of debris.

Pro Tip

Keep a shallow pan handy before checking the drain filter — it's designed to hold residual water, and opening it without one usually means a small mess on the floor.

3. Dryer Won't Heat

Check the lint trap and vent line first; a blocked vent is the most common culprit. Fresno's dry climate means lint accumulates faster here than in more humid regions.

4. Dishwasher Leaves Dishes Dirty

Clogged spray arms or a dirty filter are usually to blame. Clean both thoroughly before assuming a bigger mechanical issue.

5. Oven Won't Reach Temperature

A failing igniter or heating element is the typical cause on both gas and electric ranges. Check the door seal first, since a compromised seal can mimic a heating element problem.

6. Ice Maker Not Producing Ice

Check the water line for kinks or a frozen section before assuming the unit itself has failed. Fresno's hard water can also contribute to clogs in the line over time.

7. Garbage Disposal Humming But Not Spinning

This usually means a jam; never reach in by hand — a technician can clear it safely.

Pro Tip

Most disposals have a small hex-key reset hole on the bottom of the unit designed for manually freeing a jam from outside — check your model's manual before assuming it needs a full service call.

Seasonal Patterns Worth Knowing

These seven problems don't occur evenly throughout the year. Refrigerator cooling complaints spike during Fresno's hottest summer stretches, when compressors are working hardest against extreme heat. Dryer vent and lint-related issues tend to increase during high-laundry seasons, particularly around the winter holidays with extra guests. Being aware of these patterns can help you get ahead of the specific issue most likely to affect you at a given time of year.

Which Problem Costs the Most to Fix?

Of the seven, refrigerator cooling issues have by far the widest cost range. A simple coil cleaning costs very little, while a compressor or sealed-system repair sits at the higher end of typical appliance repair costs. This range is exactly why a proper diagnostic step matters — assuming the worst-case cost before a technician has actually identified the cause often leads homeowners to consider replacement prematurely, when the actual fix may be far simpler and cheaper than expected.

Building Your Own Quick-Reference Habit

Given how consistently these seven problems come up, it's worth keeping this list somewhere handy — a note on your phone, a bookmark on this page — so that the next time something seems off, you have an immediate starting point rather than guessing where to begin.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the most common appliance problem in Fresno homes?

Refrigerator cooling issues top the list, often tied to dust on the condenser coils given Fresno's dry, dusty climate. Washer drainage problems are a close second.

Why do washers commonly fail to drain?

Usually a clogged drain hose or filter. Both are typically quick, DIY-friendly fixes once you know where to look, and rarely indicate a deeper mechanical failure.

Is it safe to reach into a garbage disposal that's humming but not spinning?

No — never reach in by hand, even with the power off. A humming-but-not-spinning disposal usually means a jam, which a technician can clear safely using proper tools.

How do I know if my dishwasher problem is the filter or something bigger?

Start by cleaning the filter and spray arms, which resolves the majority of poor-cleaning complaints. If dishes are still coming out dirty afterward, the issue is more likely a pump or spray arm mechanism needing professional attention.

Can I fix a slow-heating oven myself?

Checking the door seal is a reasonable DIY step. A failing igniter or heating element, which is the more common underlying cause, requires replacement parts and is best handled by a licensed technician.

Do these seven problems account for most service calls, or are there other common issues?

These seven represent the large majority of what Fresno and Clovis technicians see. Other issues occur, but far less frequently, making this list a reasonable starting point for most troubleshooting situations.

Is there a seasonal pattern to which of these problems occurs most?

Refrigerator cooling issues spike in summer heat, dryer vent problems increase during high-laundry seasons like the holidays, and general wear-related issues tend to surface more in the weeks following heavy-use periods.

Which of these seven problems is generally the most expensive to fix?

Refrigerator cooling issues have the widest cost range, since the underlying cause can be anything from a simple coil cleaning to a more involved compressor or sealed-system repair, making an accurate diagnosis especially valuable before assuming the worst.
