Refrigerator & Freezer Repair

Common Problems We Repair

The issues Fresno-area homeowners call us about most often.

01

Not Cooling Properly

Warm fridge, cold freezer, or a unit that runs constantly without holding temperature.

02

Freezer Too Warm or Frosting Over

Excess frost buildup, a freezer that won't hold 0°F, or uneven cooling.

03

Leaking Water

Water pooling underneath or inside the unit, often from a clogged line or drain.

04

Ice Maker & Dispenser Problems

Slow ice production, off-tasting ice, or a water valve affected by hard water buildup.

05

Unusual Noises

Buzzing, grinding, or a loud clunk on startup that wasn't there before.

06

Temperature Inconsistency

Swings that don't respond to adjusting the dial, or a thermostat that's misreading.

07

Door Seal & Gasket Issues

A worn gasket that lets warm air in, driving up frost, noise and energy use.

08

Control & Electrical Issues

Panel or control problems affecting temperature settings and normal operation.

Refrigerator Repair FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What refrigerator problems does MVI repair?

We repair refrigerators and freezers for cooling problems, leaks, ice maker and water dispenser issues, unusual noises, temperature inconsistency, and door seal or gasket problems — the most common issues Fresno-area homeowners run into.

Do you repair Sub-Zero refrigerators?

Yes. We service Sub-Zero along with other premium brands including KitchenAid, Thermador, Monogram, Viking and Miele, as well as mainstream brands like Samsung, LG, Whirlpool, Maytag, Frigidaire and GE.

Do you repair refrigerators in Fresno, Clovis, and Madera?

Yes. We provide refrigerator repair across Fresno, Clovis and Madera, with Fresno as our home base.

How quickly can I schedule refrigerator repair?

Same-day scheduling is available whenever possible. Call or text us anytime and we'll confirm the soonest opening for your address.

What warranty comes with refrigerator repair?

Every refrigerator repair is backed by a 90-day labor warranty and a 30-day parts warranty, using certified parts.

Do you repair compressors or sealed systems?

No. MVI does not perform sealed-system or compressor repairs. If diagnosis points to one of these, we'll tell you clearly so you can make the right next decision.

Is it worth repairing an older refrigerator?

It depends on the refrigerator's age, the cost of the repair, and how that compares to replacement. Our technicians give you an honest assessment during the diagnostic visit so you can decide what makes sense for your situation.
