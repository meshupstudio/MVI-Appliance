Not Just an Inspection

We Actually Clean It

A lot of "maintenance" visits are just a quick look and a checklist. Ours aren't. When we service a dryer, we open the panel, vacuum out the lint and dust that's built up around the drum and motor, and clear the vent line — the stuff that actually causes fires and premature breakdowns.

Same goes for refrigerators, dishwashers and everything else on our maintenance list. If it's dirty, we clean it. If a part's wearing out, we tell you before it fails.

“Maintenance should leave your appliance actually cleaner, not just checked off.”
